## What is Vowelkit?

[Vowelkit](https://www.vowelkit.com) is a free word guessing game that runs in any browser, on phone or desktop. Guess the hidden word in six tries. After each guess the tiles show which letters are in the right spot, which are in the word but misplaced, and which are not in the word at all.

No account, no download and no paywall. New to the game? Read [how to play](https://www.vowelkit.com/how-to-play).

## Free word game solvers

* **[Wordle solver](https://www.vowelkit.com/wordle-solver):** enter your green, yellow and gray letters and see every word that still fits.
* **[Word unscrambler](https://www.vowelkit.com):** type a jumble of letters and get the real words you can make, grouped by length.
* **[Anagram solver](https://www.vowelkit.com/anagram-solver)** and **[word finder](https://www.vowelkit.com/word-finder)** for any set of letters.
* **[Spelling Bee solver](https://www.vowelkit.com/spelling-bee-solver)**, **[Letter Boxed solver](https://www.vowelkit.com/letter-boxed-solver)** and **[Squaredle solver](https://www.vowelkit.com/squaredle-solver)** for daily puzzle games.
* **[Word Hunt solver](https://www.vowelkit.com/word-hunt-solver)**, **[Boggle solver](https://www.vowelkit.com/boggle-solver)**, **[Text Twist solver](https://www.vowelkit.com/text-twist-solver)**, **[Jumble solver](https://www.vowelkit.com/jumble-solver)** and **[Words With Friends helper](https://www.vowelkit.com/words-with-friends-cheat)**.

## Browser extensions

* **[Vowelkit: Word Finder & Unscrambler](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/vowelkit-word-finder/)** for Firefox and [Edge](https://microsoftedge.microsoft.com/addons/detail/kaddclonconccmcncmdgplllpnmkigme): type your letters in the toolbar and see the best words, or right-click any word to check it.
